Our Story

Connecting Readers With Stories They'll Love

Midnight Reads was born from a simple frustration: too many books, too little guidance. We set out to build a platform that feels like getting a recommendation from a trusted friend who reads everything — one who genuinely knows your taste and never steers you wrong.

  • Personalised novel recommendations tailored to your genre preferences
  • Genre-based discovery pathways for new and experienced readers
  • Curated lists — Hidden Gems, Award Winners, Page-Turners, and more
  • A space to explore new authors, debut novels, and international fiction
  • A warm, welcoming community of passionate book lovers

At Midnight Reads, we believe every reader deserves a story that captivates them completely. Let us help you find the books that keep you turning pages long after midnight.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before diving in. Can't find your answer? Drop us a message anytime.

How does Midnight Reads recommend novels to me?

When you sign up, we ask about your favourite genres, authors, and the moods you read in. As you rate books and build your reading list, our recommendation engine learns your taste and gets sharper over time. The more you interact, the better your picks become — think of it as a friend who's read everything and remembers exactly what you loved.

Is Midnight Reads free to use?

Yes — our Reader plan is completely free, forever. You get unlimited genre browsing, five personalised recommendations each month, and full access to community reviews. For unlimited recommendations, reading tracking, and book club features, you can upgrade to the Book Lover plan for $9/month with a 14-day free trial.

Can I trust the community reviews to be spoiler-free?

Absolutely. Every review on Midnight Reads goes through our spoiler-tagging system. Reviewers are required to flag any major plot reveals, and our moderation team monitors for unmarked spoilers. You can browse and read reviews confidently without worrying about having key plot points revealed before you've had the chance to experience them yourself.

Do you only recommend popular or well-known novels?

Not at all — finding hidden gems is one of the things we're proudest of. While we do feature bestsellers and award winners, a significant portion of our recommendations are debut novels, international fiction, and under-the-radar titles that tend to fly under the radar on mainstream platforms. We believe great stories deserve to be found, regardless of how many copies they've sold.

How do I start a book club on Midnight Reads?

Book clubs are available on our Book Club plan ($29/month). Once subscribed, you can create a club, invite up to 20 members via email or a shareable link, set a monthly reading pick, and access dedicated discussion forums for each book. We also provide monthly curated suggestions to help your club decide what to read next if you'd like a helping hand.
