Quick Answers

Common questions before you reach out.

See all FAQs
Is Midnight Reads free to join?

Yes — our Reader plan is completely free, forever. You get unlimited genre browsing and five personalised recommendations each month.

How do recommendations work?

When you sign up, we ask about your favourite genres and authors. As you rate books, our engine learns your taste and gets sharper over time.

Do you recommend hidden gems, not just bestsellers?

Absolutely — finding hidden gems is one of the things we're proudest of. Debut novels, international fiction, and under-the-radar titles are a big part of what we do.
